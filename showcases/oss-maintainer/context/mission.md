# Mission

The open-source maintenance business keeps **acme-toolkit** (a fictional open-source
command-line tool) healthy and trusted:

- Ship predictable, well-documented releases on a monthly minor cadence.
- Keep the issue tracker triaged: no duplicate reports, every report has a clear
  owner or an explicit "unassigned" state.
- Treat community feedback as a first-class input to the roadmap.
- Stay honest: no fabricated facts, no unverifiable claims, no publishing to
  external channels from this workspace.

This business is a demo case. All project names, versions, metrics, and community
records are fictional and exist only inside this showcase directory.
